    Symmetry Jump: Mathematical Foundation

    This simulation models a system where points move through space using point reflection (symmetry).

    • The Jump: When Stone A jumps over Stone B, it reflects across B as its center. Formula: A′ = 2B − A
    • The Parity Invariant (Odd/Even Rule): The parity of a stone's coordinates can never change! Since `2B` is always an even number, `2B - A` preserves the odd/even status of `A`. An (Even, Even) stone is permanently trapped on even coordinates and can never capture an (Odd, Odd) space.
    • First Visit: The first stone to land on an unvisited coordinate earns 10 Points.
    • Area Control (Owned): The last stone to land on a coordinate claims it. The score awarded is 10 / (total visits), rewarding the exploration of new territory.
    • Real Score: The total raw score divided by the number of jumps the stone has made.

    How to Use the System

    • Setup: Click New Settings to define the grid size and stone count. You can enter coordinates manually or use Place on Grid to position them with your mouse.
    • Manual Play: Select a stone by clicking on it, then click on the target stone you want to jump over. The selected stone will perform the symmetry jump.
    • Auto Simulation: Click the Play button to let the computer run automatically. It will use a mixture of Uniform, Cryptographic, and Gaussian random algorithms to pick jumpers and pivots.
    • Heatmap: Click Toggle Heatmap to visualize the density of jumps and see which areas are most heavily contested.
